## 12:12:28 — Sebastian_BMW

[Source](https://discord.com/channels/1553151744047976549/1553306479019429939/1558421954745073665)

Your mission

Build an AI-powered application that ingests heterogeneous data, derives customer requirements for a vehicle, prioritizes them, and puts a product manager in the loop at the decisions that matter.

Your app should be able to:
•    Ingest the provided data and enrich it with structured knowledge from the web.
•    Extract signals such as recurring complaints, unmet needs, competitor advantages and market trends, each linked to its sources.
•    Derive requirements from those findings: clear, actionable, customer-facing and realistic.
•    Prioritize them with a logic you define and can explain.
•    Involve the PM to approve, reject, edit or challenge requirements and their rationale and evidence behind them.
•    Record everything in an audit trail (is a complete, chronological record of all actions, changes, and access events within a system or data environment).

## 12:12:57 — Sebastian_BMW

[Source](https://discord.com/channels/1553151744047976549/1553306479019429939/1558422075998343281)

About the Scope

In scope:
•    Customer requirements on the product: features, equipment, comfort, space, usability, infotainment and digital services, design and quality perception, driving experience, range and charging as experienced by the customer, variant and package offering, etc.

Out of scope:
•    Regulatory and homologation requirements (e.g.: safety, emissions, type approval)
•    Technical and engineering requirements (e.g.: component specs, architecture, supplier topics)
•    Pricing, business case and cost calculation (you may use cost or effort as a rough prioritization factor)

## 12:16:02 — Sebastian_BMW

[Source](https://discord.com/channels/1553151744047976549/1553306479019429939/1558422852284190811)

Expected Deliverables A concept and demonstrable prototype of an AI-powered product requirements application that processes heterogeneous inputs, derives product requirements, supports prioritization, and integrates human review. , A structured sample list of derived product requirements. Each requirement should include a unique identifier, a clear and actionable description, its linked findings and sources, a priority or score, the applied prioritization rationale, an evidence level, the key future-facing assumptions and uncertainties and its current decision status. , A clearly defined end-to-end workflow showing how inputs are processed into proposed and prioritized requirements, which activities the AI performs autonomously, and where human review is mandatory. , A demonstrable audit trail that makes each requirement traceable from its underlying findings and sources through consolidation, scoring, prioritization, and human decision-making. It should record relevant changes to requirement content, scores, priorities, evidence, and status, including who or what initiated each change, when it occurred, and the documented rationale. , Important: Teams are free to choose their technical approach, architecture, prioritization logic, and user experience. The deliverables describe the capabilities and outcomes that should be demonstrated, not a prescribed solution design. Deliverables may be presented as one integrated solution or as a combination of connected concepts, prototypes, models, and visualizations.

@everyone We just received the question what common pain points of product managers are. One certainly is to balance customer requirements across different markets and regions, as preferences differ given for instance the climate, customer usage or culture. ## 15:27:46 — Bene_BMW

[Source](https://discord.com/channels/1553151744047976549/1553306479019429939/1558471105851686952)

If you are looking for solid data to back up your product ideas for the hackathon, the BMW Annual Report is a great resource. You don't need to read the entire financial breakdown—just focus on extracting data from these two key areas to ground your product strategy:

Market & Sales Data: Look for regional growth trends, model series deliveries (e.g., X Series vs. sedans), and the latest EV adoption rates. This data is perfect for defining your target user persona, proving market demand, and prioritizing where your feature will have the biggest impact.

Product Strategy & R&D: Check the sections on R&D investment priorities, ConnectedDrive and My BMW App metrics, and the "Neue Klasse" platform strategy. Tying your concept to these areas proves that your idea is technically feasible and aligns directly with current engineering investments and our future vehicle architecture. https://www.bmwgroup.com/content/dam/grpw/websites/bmwgroup_com/ir/downloads/en/2026/bericht/BMW-Group-Report-2025-en.pdf
